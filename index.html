OpenCanvas 100%

Shapes

Click a shape to add it. Drag to move, use the corner handles to resize and the round handle to rotate. Hold Shift to keep proportions or snap rotation.

Text

Double-click text on the page to edit it. Fonts are the ones already installed on your device, so nothing is downloaded.

Images

You can also drag a picture onto the page or paste one. Images stay on your device and never leave the browser.

Templates

Templates replace the current design.

Design with Claude

Optional. Describe what you want and Claude edits the page for you. This needs your own Anthropic API key; the editor itself never needs an account.

Your key is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Claude sees the layout of your page (text, sizes, colors), not your uploaded pictures.

Page
